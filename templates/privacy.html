Privacy Policy

Your financial data is personal. This policy explains what Spendly collects, how we use it, and how we keep it safe.

Last updated · October 5, 2026

01

Data We Collect

We only collect what we need to run the service:

  • Account details, such as your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense records you enter, including amount, category, date, and description.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and session data, needed to keep you signed in.
02

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve Spendly. This includes:

  • Displaying your expenses, category breakdowns, and summaries.
  • Authenticating you and keeping your account secure.
  • Diagnosing problems and improving the reliability of the service.

We never sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

03

Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and you may request deletion of your account and all associated records at any time.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

04

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of trusted third-party providers, such as hosting and font delivery services, to operate. These providers only receive the minimum information required to perform their function and are not permitted to use it for any other purpose.

05

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to access, correct, or delete your data, contact us at privacy@example.com.
